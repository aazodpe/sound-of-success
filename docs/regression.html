Regression

Overview

Regression fits a relationship between inputs and an outcome and reports how strong each contribution is. Linear regression predicts a number; logistic regression predicts the probability of a yes-or-no outcome.

Popularity is scored on a continuous scale, which makes it a natural regression target — how much popularity does a song gain per unit of danceability, holding the rest constant? Charting, by contrast, is a yes-or-no outcome, and logistic regression gives a calibrated probability for it rather than a bare verdict.
