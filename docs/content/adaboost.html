AdaBoost

Overview

AdaBoost builds a long line of very simple models, each one trained to pay most attention to the cases the previous models got wrong. No single member of that line is any good on its own; the strength comes from how their votes are weighted and combined.

The appeal here is that it keeps re-weighting toward the hard cases. If the songs that break the pattern are a small, distinctive group rather than random noise, a method that deliberately chases its own mistakes should expose them, and the examples it struggles with longest are worth looking at directly.
