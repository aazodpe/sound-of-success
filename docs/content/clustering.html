Clustering

Overview

Clustering looks for natural groupings in data without being told what the groups are. Each song becomes a point in a space defined by its measurable qualities — how danceable it is, how loud, how fast, how acoustic — and the algorithm asks which songs sit close together.

The interesting question here is whether the clusters the data forms on its own line up with the genre labels the industry uses. If a clustering algorithm that has never seen the word “country” still separates country songs from electronic ones, that says genre is audible. If the clusters cut across genre lines instead, that says the labels are more about marketing and audience than about sound.
