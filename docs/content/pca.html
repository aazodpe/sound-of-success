PCA

Overview

A song in this dataset is described by a dozen or so numbers at once, which is more dimensions than anyone can picture. PCA finds the few combined directions along which songs differ most, so the whole collection can be viewed in two or three dimensions without throwing away much of what made the songs different.

PCA will be used both to visualize the song space and to ask which qualities actually carry the variation. If energy, loudness and acousticness turn out to move together as a single underlying factor, that factor — not the individual measurements — is the real axis along which popular music varies.
